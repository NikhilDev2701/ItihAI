# Victoria Memorial

## Overview
The Victoria Memorial is a grand marble building located in central Kolkata (Calcutta), West Bengal, India. Built between 1906 and 1921 amidst 64 acres of landscaped gardens, it serves as a premier museum and the primary cultural landmark of the city.

## Historical Background
Conceived by Lord Curzon, Viceroy of India, following the death of Queen Victoria in January 1901. Curzon envisioned a grand memorial hall to commemorate the monarch and display the history of the British Raj in India. The foundation stone was laid by the Prince of Wales (later King George V) in 1906, and the building was formally inaugurated by the Prince of Wales (later Duke of Windsor) in December 1921.

## Cultural Significance
While originally built as an imperial monument, the Victoria Memorial has evolved into an integral civic and cultural hub for Kolkata. It houses the most important museum collection of 18th-to-20th-century paintings, archival documents, and regional artworks documenting Bengal and modern Indian history.

## Architecture
- **Indo-Saracenic Revivalist Style**: Designed by Sir William Emerson, President of the Royal Institute of British Architects, blending British Classical and Italian Renaissance forms with Mughal domes and Saracenic arches.
- **Makrana Marble**: Constructed using the same white marble quarried from Makrana in Rajasthan that was used for the Taj Mahal.
- **Angel of Victory**: A 16-foot bronze sculpture weighing 3 tons mounted on a ball-bearing mechanism atop the central dome, which rotates with the wind.

## Important Facts
- The memorial houses the world's largest single collection of landscape oil paintings by Thomas and William Daniell.
- It holds rare manuscripts, including original copies of the *Ain-i-Akbari* and miniature Mughal paintings.
- The 64-acre surrounding estate features 21 historic water pools and over 2,800 trees.

## Local Traditions
- **Kolkata Adda & Morning Walks**: The lush grounds host daily intellectual discussions (*Adda*), artists, and thousands of morning exercisers.
- **Cultural Exhibitions & Book Festivals**: Regular national art retrospectives and literary events in the museum galleries.

## Nearby Attractions
- **St. Paul's Cathedral** (600 m): First Anglican cathedral built in Asia with Gothic revival architecture.
- **Indian Museum** (1.5 km): Founded in 1814, the oldest and largest multidisciplinary museum in India.
- **Park Street** (1.2 km): Kolkata's historic dining and cultural boulevard.

## Visitor Information
- **Best Time to Visit**: October to March.
- **Hours**: Museum Galleries: 10:00 AM – 6:00 PM (Closed Mondays & National Holidays); Gardens: 5:30 AM – 6:00 PM daily.
- **Foreign Tourist Entry**: ₹500 (Composite pass for museum & gardens).
- **Dress Code**: Smart casual.
- **Photography**: Permitted in the gardens; non-flash permitted in specified museum corridors.
