# ItihAI Knowledge Base — Authoritative Data Sources & References

This document records the official, authoritative sources used to compile verified historical, architectural, and cultural data across all heritage landmarks in the ItihAI platform.

---

## 🏛️ Primary Institutional Sources

### 1. Archaeological Survey of India (ASI)
- **Organization**: Ministry of Culture, Government of India
- **URL**: [https://asi.nic.in](https://asi.nic.in)
- **Scope**: Monument protection records, architectural surveys, excavation reports, visitor rules, and conservation chronicles.
- **Sites Covered**: Taj Mahal, Red Fort, Qutub Minar, Konark Sun Temple, Bishnupur Temples, Sanchi Stupa, Ajanta Caves, Ellora Caves, Hampi, Fatehpur Sikri, Mahabodhi Temple, Khajuraho.

### 2. UNESCO World Heritage Centre
- **Organization**: United Nations Educational, Scientific and Cultural Organization
- **URL**: [https://whc.unesco.org](https://whc.unesco.org)
- **Scope**: Outstanding Universal Value (OUV) statements, nomination dossiers, boundary maps, and conservation criteria.
- **Sites Covered**: All inscribed UNESCO World Heritage monuments in the ItihAI repository.

### 3. Ministry of Culture & National Portal of India
- **Organization**: Government of India
- **URL**: [https://www.indiaculture.gov.in](https://www.indiaculture.gov.in)
- **Scope**: Indian cultural heritage archives, festival documentation, intangible cultural heritage registers.

### 4. Victoria Memorial Hall Authority & Ministry of Culture
- **Organization**: Board of Trustees, Victoria Memorial Hall
- **URL**: [https://www.victoriamemorial-cal.org](https://www.victoriamemorial-cal.org)
- **Scope**: Archival records, art collections, architectural history of Kolkata colonial landmark.

### 5. Bodhgaya Temple Management Committee (BTMC)
- **Organization**: Bodhgaya Temple Management Committee & Bihar State Tourism
- **URL**: [https://bodhgaya.org](https://bodhgaya.org)
- **Scope**: Mahabodhi Temple historical records, Bodhi Tree lineage, Buddhist spiritual traditions and pilgrimage guidelines.

## 📜 Verification Principles
1. **Authoritative Primacy**: Only government archaeological records, international heritage bodies (UNESCO), and academic primary sources are accepted. Unverified blogs and hearsay are excluded.
2. **Conservative Historical Fact-Checking**: When historical dates or attributions have scholarly divergence, conservative ranges and consensus chronologies are documented rather than speculative assertions.
3. **No Copyright Infringement**: All textual narratives are original, human-synthesized summaries based on factual archaeological data.
4. **RAG Readiness**: Structured headings ensure future vector chunking (in Phase 6) produces clean semantic boundaries for precise retrieval.
